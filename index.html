HOW TO PLAY

You are a mote of light surrounded by the dark. Survive as long as you can.

Move around — your weapons fire on their own. Kill enemies to drop cyan XP motes; walk over them (or rely on your magnet pull) to fill the XP bar. Each level lets you pick one upgrade from three random cards — either a new weapon, a weapon level-up, or a passive boost.

→
